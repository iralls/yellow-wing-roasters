The Problem

White Bird, Light Background

The snowy egret disappears on #f0f2f5. Here are several ways to fix it — shown with both the egret and the robin (which doesn't have the problem) for comparison.

Option A

Drop Shadow

A subtle drop shadow on all bird images. Light birds get a visible edge, dark birds look slightly lifted. No markup changes needed — CSS only.
